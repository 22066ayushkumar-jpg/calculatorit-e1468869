How much are vehicle registration fees in Arkansas?

The base annual registration fee collected by the Arkansas Department of Finance and Administration (DFA) is very affordable, ranging strictly from $17.00 to $30.00 based on the unladen weight of your passenger vehicle. The state also adds a $2.50 validation decal fee and a $0.50 postage fee for mail/online renewals. However, alternative fuel vehicles face significant annual surcharges, and every driver must pay a county personal property tax before the DFA allows them to renew.

What affects your Arkansas registration fee?

  • Vehicle Weight: Passenger cars weighing 3,000 lbs or less pay $17.00 per year. Vehicles between 3,001 and 4,500 lbs pay $25.00. Vehicles 4,501 lbs and over pay $30.00. Standard pickup trucks have a flat rate of $21.00.
  • Alternative Fuel Surcharges: To offset declining gas tax revenues, Arkansas imposes a $200 annual fee for all-electric vehicles (BEVs). Plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEVs) pay a $100 annual fee. Standard hybrids (HEVs) pay a $50 annual fee.
  • State Sales Tax Threshold: If you are purchasing a vehicle with a net price of $4,000 or more, you must pay the state sales tax (6.5%). Vehicles sold for under $4,000 are completely exempt from the state sales tax portion.
  • County Personal Property Tax: Required annually, this tax is assessed at 20% of your vehicle's NADA Clean Trade-In value, multiplied by the local millage rate. It is usually the largest ongoing expense for an Arkansas driver.

How are registration fees calculated in Arkansas?

The total checkout price for your Arkansas DFA transaction is calculated using the following basic formula:

  • (Base Weight Fee) + (Validation Decal: $2.50) + (Postage: $0.50) + (EV/Hybrid Surcharge if applicable).

If you are registering a newly purchased car, you must add the $10.00 Certificate of Title fee, a $0.50 lien filing fee if financed, and the combined sales tax on your net purchase price.

What this calculator may not include

  • Exact County Property Taxes: The property tax relies entirely on your specific county's millage rates and the vehicle's depreciating fair market value. It must be estimated.
  • Local Sales Tax Caps: While the state sales tax is flat, local city and county sales taxes are often capped at a maximum of $25 per 1% of the local tax rate. This calculator provides a straightforward combined percentage approximation.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Arkansas?

The DFA registration fee for a passenger vehicle ranges from $17 to $30 annually, plus a $3 decal and postage fee. However, you must also pay a hefty county personal property tax before the DFA will allow you to renew.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Arkansas?

This calculator precisely maps standard passenger cars, light pickup trucks, and motorcycles. It does not calculate specific fees for commercial trucks (which range up to $1,350), farm trucks, mobile homes, or motorized bicycles.

Can registration fees in Arkansas change every year?

Your base state registration fee is a flat rate based on unladen weight and does not depreciate over time. However, your county personal property tax bill will decrease annually as the vehicle depreciates in market value.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Arkansas?

Yes. If you are registering a newly acquired vehicle, select "New Purchase." This will calculate your mandatory $10 Title Fee and your exact sales tax based on the net purchase price. Remember, Arkansas exempts vehicles priced under $4,000 from state sales tax.
