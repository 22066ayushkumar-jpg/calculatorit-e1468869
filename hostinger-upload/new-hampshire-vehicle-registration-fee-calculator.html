How much are vehicle registration fees in New Hampshire?

There is no single flat fee for vehicle registration in New Hampshire. Instead, your annual bill is split into a State Fee (based on your vehicle's gross weight) and a Town/City Permit Fee (based on the vehicle's original MSRP and age) [INDEX_1.1.1, INDEX_1.1.3]. For an average modern sedan (3,500 lbs) that is 3 years old with an original MSRP of $30,000, you would pay a $43.20 state fee plus a $360.00 town fee, bringing the total to roughly $415 per year (including local administrative surcharges) [INDEX_1.1.3, INDEX_1.1.5].

What affects your New Hampshire registration fee?

  • Original MSRP (List Price): Your municipal permit fee is derived directly from the manufacturer's original list price [INDEX_1.1.1]. New Hampshire has no sales tax on vehicles; this MSRP-based property tax acts as the state's primary vehicle revenue source.
  • Vehicle Age: The town permit fee depreciates significantly. A brand new car is taxed at a rate of 18 mills ($18 per $1,000 of MSRP). By its 6th year, that rate drops to the minimum of 3 mills ($3 per $1,000) [INDEX_1.1.2, INDEX_1.2.2].
  • Vehicle Weight: The state DMV portion of the bill is based on weight. Vehicles under 3,000 lbs cost $31.20, those between 3,001 and 5,000 lbs cost $43.20, and vehicles up to 8,000 lbs cost $55.20 [INDEX_1.1.3].
  • Your Birth Month: In NH, plates expire during the primary owner's birth month [INDEX_1.1.1]. If you buy a new car, your first registration will be prorated to align with your birth month, meaning your first bill could represent anywhere from 5 to 16 months of fees [INDEX_1.1.1, INDEX_1.1.2].

How are registration fees calculated in New Hampshire?

Your local Town or City Clerk handles both portions of the calculation simultaneously. The formulas used are:

  • State Weight Fee: Fixed tiers ($31.20, $43.20, $55.20) for standard light vehicles [INDEX_1.1.3].
  • Town Permit Fee: (Original MSRP ÷ 1000) × Millage Rate [INDEX_1.1.2]
    • Year 1: 18 mills ($0.018)
    • Year 2: 15 mills ($0.015)
    • Year 3: 12 mills ($0.012)
    • Year 4: 9 mills ($0.009)
    • Year 5: 6 mills ($0.006)
    • Year 6+: 3 mills ($0.003)

Finally, mandatory processing fees are added, including a $3.00 Municipal Agent fee, a $2.00 Town Clerk prep fee, and potentially a $25 title application fee if you are registering a newly purchased car [INDEX_1.1.3, INDEX_1.1.5].

What this calculator may not include

  • Proration: As mentioned, a new registration will be prorated (5 to 16 months) to sync with your birth month [INDEX_1.1.1, INDEX_1.1.2]. This tool outputs a clean 12-month annual estimate for budgeting purposes.
  • Specialty / Vanity Plates: Choosing a vanity plate adds a $40.00 annual state fee plus a one-time $8.00 plate manufacturing fee [INDEX_1.1.3].
  • Emissions & Safety Inspections: New Hampshire requires an annual safety inspection (and an OBD emissions test for vehicles 1996 and newer), which is paid directly to an authorized mechanic, not the Town Clerk.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in New Hampshire?

Your total fee is a combination of a weight-based State fee ($31.20 to $55.20 for most cars) and an MSRP-based Town permit fee. Because the town fee charges $18 per $1,000 of MSRP on a new car, your total bill can easily reach $600 to $900 in the first year [INDEX_1.1.1, INDEX_1.1.2, INDEX_1.1.3].

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in New Hampshire?

This calculator is accurate for standard passenger vehicles, SUVs, and light trucks under 8,000 lbs using the standard 12-month DMV and Municipal schedules [INDEX_1.1.2, INDEX_1.1.3]. It does not calculate heavy commercial fleet registrations, apportioned plates, or trailer specific fees [INDEX_1.1.3].

Can registration fees in New Hampshire change every year?

Yes. Because the Town Permit fee depreciates over a 6-year schedule, your registration bill will decrease every year until the vehicle is 6 years old, at which point the municipal tax bottoms out at 3 mills ($3 per $1,000 of original MSRP) [INDEX_1.1.2, INDEX_1.2.2].

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in New Hampshire?

Yes. If you are registering a newly purchased car, select "New Purchase" to include the required state title application and license plate issuance fees [INDEX_1.1.3, INDEX_1.1.5]. Make sure you enter the original window sticker MSRP regardless of whether the car is new or used [INDEX_1.1.1, INDEX_1.1.2].
