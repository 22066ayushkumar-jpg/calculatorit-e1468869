How much are vehicle registration fees in New Jersey?

Standard passenger vehicle registration in New Jersey typically ranges between $46.50 and $84.00 per year depending on the vehicle's weight and model year. However, if you are purchasing a brand new car, New Jersey requires a mandatory 4-year upfront registration, meaning your initial checkout fee will be four times the annual rate (e.g., up to $336 for a heavy SUV). If you drive a battery electric vehicle, you must also pay a new annual EV surcharge, which adds an additional $270 per year to your total in 2026.

What affects your New Jersey registration fee?

  • Vehicle Weight Class: The NJ Motor Vehicle Commission (MVC) uses three main weight classes for passenger cars: under 2,700 lbs, 2,700 - 3,800 lbs, and over 3,800 lbs. Heavier vehicles cost more.
  • Vehicle Age: Vehicles under two years old pay a slightly higher base registration fee than vehicles older than two years.
  • The 4-Year Mandate: Brand new passenger vehicles (never titled before) must pay for four years of registration upfront. This also applies to the EV fee, meaning a new EV buyer will pay $1,080 in EV fees at the time of purchase.
  • Electric Vehicle Surcharge: Implemented recently to fund road maintenance, the annual BEV fee rises to $270 in July 2026. Plug-in hybrids (PHEVs) are exempt from this specific surcharge because they still pay gas tax.
  • Sales Tax and Trade-Ins: New Jersey charges a statewide 6.625% sales tax on car purchases. If you have a trade-in, that value is deducted from the purchase price before the tax is calculated, reducing your overall bill.

How are registration fees calculated in New Jersey?

The calculation formula for passenger vehicles in NJ checks the weight class and age tier to determine the annual rate, and then multiplies it by the registration term (1 or 4 years).

  • Light (Under 2,700 lbs): $59 (< 2 yrs old) or $46.50 (2+ yrs old)
  • Mid (2,700 - 3,800 lbs): $71.50 (< 2 yrs old) or $59.00 (2+ yrs old)
  • Heavy (Over 3,800 lbs): $84.00 (< 2 yrs old) or $71.50 (2+ yrs old)

If a new title is required, the MVC charges $60 for a clean title, $85 if one lien is recorded, and $110 for two liens.

What this calculator may not include

  • County/Municipal Fees: Certain NJ municipalities have special parking or local fees, though state registration is generally uniform.
  • Specialty Plates: Dedicated license plates (e.g., sports teams, Shore Conservation, personalized tags) have a $50 initial fee and a $10 annual renewal fee on top of your base registration.
  • Lease Taxes: If you are leasing instead of financing/buying, New Jersey calculates sales tax on the total sum of the lease payments rather than the total price of the car.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in New Jersey?

A standard 1-year renewal for a passenger vehicle ranges from $46.50 to $84.00 based on its weight. However, brand new cars require a 4-year upfront payment, meaning your initial registration will cost between $236 and $336.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in New Jersey?

This calculator is accurate for standard passenger cars, SUVs, and motorcycles. It does not calculate commercial vehicle fees (which are based on gross vehicle weight ratings up to 80,000 lbs) or commuter vans.

Can registration fees in New Jersey change every year?

Your base registration fee will drop slightly once your vehicle becomes more than two years old. Additionally, if you drive a battery electric vehicle, the state mandated EV fee increases by $10 each July until it caps out at $290 in 2028.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in New Jersey?

Yes. Just make sure to select "Brand New Vehicle (Mandatory 4-Year Reg)" if the car has never been titled before, so the calculator can accurately apply the 4-year multiplier. For a used car purchase, select "Under 2 Years Old" or "2+ Years Old" along with the "Title Transfer" transaction type.
