How much are vehicle registration fees in Wisconsin?

The base registration fee for a standard automobile in Wisconsin is $85.00 per year. However, this $85 rate acts as a "floor," not the final bill. If you live in a district that levies a municipal and/or county "Wheel Tax," or if you drive an electric vehicle, your annual renewal will be significantly higher. For example, a driver in Madison pays their $85 base fee plus an $80 combined wheel tax ($40 county + $40 city), totaling $165.00 per year.

What affects your Wisconsin registration fee?

  • Vehicle Type and Weight: Passenger automobiles have a flat $85 fee, while light trucks are billed based on their gross weight. Trucks up to 6,000 lbs cost $100 per year, and trucks between 6,001 and 8,000 lbs cost $106 per year. Motorcycles cost $23 and are registered on a biennial (two-year) basis.
  • Local Wheel Tax: 14 counties and over 50 municipalities in Wisconsin currently charge a local wheel tax to fund their own road maintenance. The Wisconsin Department of Transportation collects this fee during your initial registration and each subsequent renewal.
  • Alternative Fuel Surcharges: Because owners of EVs and hybrids do not pay the same amount of state gas taxes to maintain Wisconsin roads, the state imposes mandatory surcharges. Fully electric vehicles are subject to a $175 annual fee, while hybrid vehicles face a $75 annual fee.
  • Titling and Taxes: When purchasing a new or used vehicle, you must pay a massive $164.50 Certificate of Title fee to put the car in your name. The state also charges a 5.0% sales tax, plus any local county or city sales taxes based on where the vehicle will be customarily kept. Wisconsin thankfully allows you to deduct the full value of any trade-in allowance from your taxable purchase price.

How are registration fees calculated in Wisconsin?

The total checkout price for your WisDOT transaction is calculated using the following basic formula:

  • (Base State Fee) + (County Wheel Tax) + (Municipal Wheel Tax) + (EV/Hybrid Surcharge if applicable).

If you are registering a newly purchased car, you must add the $164.50 Title Fee, an approximate $8 to $12 fee for new plates, and the combined sales tax on your net purchase price.

What this calculator may not include

  • Dealer Documentation Fees: The fees calculated here go exclusively to the state and local governments. Any documentation or preparation fees charged by a dealership are separate and legally negotiable.
  • Late Registration Penalties: Renewing your registration after it expires carries an automatic $10 late fee from WisDOT.
  • Heavy Commercial Trucks: Truck tractors and buses scaling up to 80,000 lbs have an exponentially larger fee schedule and are completely excluded from this passenger-focused calculator.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Wisconsin?

The state floor for passenger cars is $85 per year. However, local county and municipal wheel taxes, along with alternative fuel surcharges, frequently push this amount well over $150 or $200 for many drivers.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Wisconsin?

This calculator precisely maps standard passenger cars, light trucks under 8,000 lbs, and motorcycles. It does not calculate specific fees for motor homes, dual purpose farm vehicles, or heavy commercial fleets which utilize separate weight-specific charts.

Can registration fees in Wisconsin change every year?

Your base state registration fee is flat and does not depreciate over time based on the age or value of your vehicle. The only variance occurs if your local municipality or county introduces a new Wheel Tax, as the list of participating cities continually grows.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Wisconsin?

Yes. If you are registering a newly acquired vehicle (new from a dealer or used from a private party), select "New Purchase." This will calculate your mandatory $164.50 Title Fee and your exact sales tax based on the net purchase price.
