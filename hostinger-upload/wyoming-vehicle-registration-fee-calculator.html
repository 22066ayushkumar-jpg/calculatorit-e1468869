How much are vehicle registration fees in Wyoming?

In Wyoming, your annual registration fee is split into two parts: a flat state fee and a county fee based on the value of your vehicle. The state fee is very cheap—typically just $30 for passenger vehicles and $25 for motorcycles. However, the county fee (ad valorem tax) can range from a few dollars for an old car to over $1,000 for a brand-new luxury truck. If you drive an electric vehicle, you will also pay a $100 annual surcharge (reduced from $200 effective July 2026).

What affects your Wyoming registration fee?

  • Original MSRP: The county portion of your registration is an ad valorem tax based strictly on the vehicle's original Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (factory cost), not what you currently paid for it.
  • Vehicle Age (Year of Service): Wyoming depreciates the factory cost heavily over six years. In its first year, the vehicle is taxed on 60% of its MSRP. By its sixth year, it bottoms out at 15%.
  • Alternative Fuel Surcharges: Starting in July 2026, Wyoming lowered its electric vehicle (BEV) fee to $100 per year, but introduced a new $50 annual fee for plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEVs).
  • Sales Tax: If titling a new purchase, you must pay the state 4% sales tax plus any local county option taxes (which often brings the total to 5% or 7% depending on the county). Wyoming subtracts your trade-in allowance from the taxable amount before calculation.

How are registration fees calculated in Wyoming?

The Wyoming County Treasurer calculates your renewal utilizing this specific statutory formula:

  • County Fee: MSRP × Year of Service Rate × 0.03
    • Service Rates: Year 1 (60%), Year 2 (50%), Year 3 (40%), Year 4 (30%), Year 5 (20%), Year 6+ (15%).
  • State Fee: Flat $30 for passenger cars/trucks, or $25 for motorcycles.
  • Total: County Fee + State Fee + EV/PHEV Surcharge (if applicable).

What this calculator may not include

  • Specialty/Vanity Plates: Embossed, personalized, or university plates carry additional upfront and annual fees.

  • Commercial/Heavy Trucks: Vehicles with high gross combination weights face a vastly different state fee schedule that scales up to $1,548+.

FAQS

How much is the car registration fee in Wyoming?

It depends on your vehicle's factory MSRP and age. The state fee is only $30, but the county tax can add hundreds of dollars. For example, a 3-year-old car with a $30,000 original MSRP will cost around $390 per year.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Wyoming?

This calculator provides a highly accurate estimate for standard passenger cars, SUVs, light trucks, and motorcycles using the official Wyoming statutory formula. It does not calculate fees for heavy commercial vehicles or trailers.

Can registration fees in Wyoming change every year?

Yes, in your favor! Your county fee will decrease every year for the first six years of the vehicle's life as the state's "Year of Service" depreciation rate drops from 60% down to 15%.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Wyoming?

Yes. Select "New Purchase" to automatically include the 4% to 7% state and county sales tax based on your net purchase price. If you are just renewing your current plates, select "Annual Tag Renewal."
