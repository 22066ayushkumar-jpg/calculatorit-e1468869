How much are vehicle registration fees in Montana?

Montana determines your registration fee using a strict age bracket system rather than a flat statewide rate. For a standard passenger vehicle or light truck (under 1 ton), the state base fees are: $217 for vehicles 0–4 years old; $87 for vehicles 5–10 years old; and $28 for vehicles 11 years and older. If your vehicle is 11 years or older, you also have the option to pay a one-time $87.50 permanent registration fee to completely age out of the annual renewal system.

What affects your Montana registration fee?

  • Vehicle Age: The biggest factor by far. A brand new car will cost nearly $200 more in base state fees than a 6-year-old car.
  • Original MSRP (The Luxury Tax): If your light vehicle has an original MSRP of $150,000 or more, Montana applies a steep $825 luxury fee every single year until the vehicle turns 11 years old. (For motorhomes, the threshold is $300,000 with an $800 fee).
  • Powertrain and Weight: To offset road wear from heavier vehicles that don't pay gas taxes, EVs are assessed a $130 (under 6,000 lbs) or $190 (over 6,000 lbs) annual surcharge. Plug-in hybrids pay $70 or $100 depending on weight.
  • County Option Tax: Almost all Montana counties (except a few like Flathead and Richland) assess a local option tax of up to 0.7% on the vehicle's depreciated retail value.

How are registration fees calculated in Montana?

Your local county treasurer combines the state's flat age-based fee with a depreciated value assessment to calculate your final bill.

  • Formula: (State Age Bracket Fee) + (State Admin & Highway Patrol Fees) + (MSRP × Depreciation % × County Tax Rate) + EV/Luxury Surcharges.

The state uses a sliding scale for depreciation, usually dropping the taxable value of the car down to roughly 10-20% of its original MSRP by the time it reaches a decade old.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Montana?

It depends entirely on the vehicle's age. Light vehicles 0-4 years old pay a $217 base fee, 5-10 years old pay $87, and 11+ years old pay $28. This base fee does not include mandatory highway patrol fees, county taxes, or EV surcharges.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Montana?

This calculator is accurate for standard passenger cars, light trucks (under 1 ton), and SUVs [INDEX_2.2.2]. It does not calculate fees for heavy commercial trucks (which use Gross Vehicle Weight tables), motorhomes, ATVs, or watercraft.

Can registration fees in Montana change every year?

Yes. Your fee will drop significantly when your vehicle crosses the 5-year and 11-year age thresholds [INDEX_2.2.3]. Furthermore, your local County Option Tax will decrease annually as your vehicle's assessed value depreciates.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Montana?

Yes. Just select the correct "Vehicle Age Bracket" to apply the correct state base fee and approximate the depreciation. If you are registering a car for the first time in your name, be sure to select "New Purchase (Initial Title & Registration)" to include the standard titling fees.
