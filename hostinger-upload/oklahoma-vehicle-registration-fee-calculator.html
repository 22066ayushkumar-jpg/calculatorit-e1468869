How much are vehicle registration fees in Oklahoma?

In Oklahoma, your base passenger vehicle registration fee relies on a scheduled depreciation model. If you buy a brand new vehicle, your annual tag renewal starts at $96.00 (plus a mandatory $1.50 insurance verification fee). However, the state rewards you for keeping the vehicle longer. Once the vehicle enters its 5th year, the rate drops to $86.00. It drops again in the 9th year ($66), the 13th year ($46), and maxes out at a low minimum of $26 per year once the vehicle is 17 years old.

What affects your Oklahoma registration fee?

  • Vehicle Age: The exact year your vehicle was manufactured dictates which of the five registration depreciation brackets you fall into.
  • Powertrain and Weight: To make up for lost fuel taxes, Oklahoma imposes the DRIVE Act surcharge on alternative fuel vehicles. If your vehicle is 6,000 lbs or below, fully electric vehicles (BEVs) pay a $110 surcharge, and plug-in hybrids (PHEVs) pay $82. If the vehicle weighs between 6,001 and 10,000 lbs, those fees jump to $158 for BEVs and $118 for PHEVs.
  • New Vehicle Taxes (Excise & Sales): When titling a newly purchased car, you must pay the Oklahoma Motor Vehicle Excise Tax (3.25%) combined with the State Motor Vehicle Sales Tax (1.25%), resulting in an effective 4.5% combined tax rate.
  • The HB 1183 Law Change (July 2026): Taking effect on July 1, 2026, Oklahoma law HB 1183 simplifies the excise tax calculation. The state removes the old 20% average retail adjustments, meaning your excise tax is now calculated strictly on the actual sales price of the vehicle.

How are registration fees calculated in Oklahoma?

Service Oklahoma uses a straightforward formula for renewals: your bracketed age fee plus the $1.50 insurance verification fee. If you have an EV or PHEV, the weight-based surcharge is added.

If you are calculating a new purchase, you must factor in the $11 Title Fee, a $17 Transfer/Owner Change Fee, and the 4.5% combined tax on the net purchase price of the vehicle.

What this calculator may not include

  • Iconic Oklahoma Plate Upgrades: Starting in September 2024, drivers have the option to upgrade to the new "Iconic Oklahoma" license plate. Doing so adds a $4 plate replacement fee during renewal, or $9 outside of renewal.
  • Commercial & Farm Tags: This calculator applies to standard non-commercial passenger vehicles. Farm vehicles have heavily discounted flat rates (approx. $30), while heavy commercial vehicles scale over $1,000 depending on gross weight.
  • Lien Processing Fees: If you finance your vehicle, the lender may require the tag agent to record a lien on the title, which carries an additional fee (typically around $10 to $15).

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Oklahoma?

A standard gas passenger vehicle registration ranges from a high of $96.00 for brand new cars down to just $26.00 for vehicles 17 years and older.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Oklahoma?

It is highly accurate for passenger cars, SUVs, and light trucks. It does not calculate specific fees for boats, outboard motors, manufactured homes, ATVs, or heavy commercial fleets.

Can registration fees in Oklahoma change every year?

Yes, but in your favor! Oklahoma operates on a depreciation schedule, so your base vehicle registration fee will physically drop during the 5th, 9th, 13th, and 17th years of the vehicle's lifespan.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Oklahoma?

Yes. Simply select "New Purchase" to automatically calculate the mandatory $28 in title and transfer fees, along with the 4.5% combined state vehicle tax applied to your net purchase price.
