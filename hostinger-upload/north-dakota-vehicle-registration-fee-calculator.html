How much are vehicle registration fees in North Dakota?

For standard passenger vehicles, North Dakota registration fees range from $41.00 to over $274.00 per year. This wide range exists because the state calculates your fee based on your vehicle's weight and the year it was first registered. A brand new, heavy SUV will cost significantly more than a 15-year-old compact car. First-time registrations also require a $5 title fee, license plate fees, and the state's 5% Motor Vehicle Excise Tax.

What affects your North Dakota registration fee?

  • Vehicle Weight: North Dakota divides passenger vehicles into several weight classes. The base fee for a vehicle under 3,200 lbs is substantially lower than one weighing over 6,000 lbs.
  • Vehicle Age: The state rewards you for keeping older cars on the road. The registration fee drops as your vehicle ages, moving through five depreciation brackets (Years 1-3, 4-6, 7-9, 10-12, and 13+).
  • Powertrain / Fuel Type: To compensate for gas tax revenues used for road maintenance, North Dakota charges an annual road use fee for alternative fuel vehicles. Fully electric vehicles (EVs) pay a $120 annual surcharge, while plug-in hybrids (PHEVs) pay $50. Electric motorcycles pay $20.
  • Motor Vehicle Excise Tax (MVET): If you are titling a newly purchased car, you must pay a 5% excise tax on the net purchase price. Any trade-in allowance is deducted from the taxable amount, saving you money.

How are registration fees calculated in North Dakota?

The NDDOT utilizes a specific matrix that intersects your vehicle's shipping weight with its first-year-of-registration age bracket.

  • Formula: Base Weight/Age Registration Fee + EV/PHEV Surcharge (if applicable) + Plate Fees = Annual Registration Cost.

For new purchases, the checkout total also includes the 5% MVET and $5 Certificate of Title fee.

FAQS

How much is the car registration fee in North Dakota?

The base registration fee varies strictly by age and weight, typically spanning from a low of $41 for older, lightweight cars up to $274 for brand new, very heavy passenger vehicles.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in North Dakota?

This tool provides a highly accurate estimate for standard passenger cars, SUVs, and light trucks based on the NDDOT weight and age fee matrix. It does not calculate commercial fleet fees, farm truck schedules, or apportioned registrations.

Can registration fees in North Dakota change every year?

Your registration fee will decrease several times over the life of your vehicle. The NDDOT fee schedule drops your base fee when your vehicle enters its 4th, 7th, 10th, and 13th years of registration.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in North Dakota?

Yes. For newly purchased vehicles, select "New Purchase" to automatically calculate the required 5% Motor Vehicle Excise Tax (MVET) and the $5 title fee. For a used car you already own, simply select "Annual Tag Renewal" and pick the correct age bracket to estimate your lowered registration cost.
