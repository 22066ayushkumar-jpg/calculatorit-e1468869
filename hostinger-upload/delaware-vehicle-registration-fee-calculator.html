How much are vehicle registration fees in Delaware?

Delaware's base registration fee is highly affordable at just $40.00 per year for standard passenger cars, SUVs, and light trucks under 5,000 lbs. Motorcycles cost just $15.00 per year. However, the state recently implemented significant road-use surcharges for alternative fuel vehicles. Fully electric vehicles (BEVs) weighing 6,000 lbs or less are charged an additional $110.00 per year, bringing their effective registration total to $150.00 annually.

What affects your Delaware registration fee?

  • Alternative Fuel Vehicle (AFV) Surcharges: Implemented via HB 164 for 2026, Delaware requires extra annual fees to replace lost gas tax revenue. Standard hybrids pay an extra $60/yr. Plug-in hybrids (PHEVs) pay $85/yr. All-electric vehicles (BEVs) pay $110/yr.
  • Motor Vehicle Document Fee: Delaware does not charge a general sales tax. Instead, when buying a vehicle, you must pay a "Document Fee," which increased from 4.25% to 5.25% of the purchase price effective late 2025.
  • Registration Term: You have the option to register a standard vehicle for multiple years (up to 7 years for brand-new vehicles). Choosing a multi-year term simply multiplies your base rate and AFV surcharge by the number of years selected.
  • Titling & Plate Fees: If you are financing a new purchase, a title with a recorded lien costs $55.00, whereas a title without a lien is $35.00. Issuing a new plate and sticker costs $10.00, while retaining and transferring an existing tag costs $20.00.

How are registration fees calculated in Delaware?

The Delaware DMV uses a straightforward flat-rate calculation devoid of local county or municipal property tax add-ons.

  • Renewal Formula: (Base Registration Fee) + (Alternative Fuel Surcharge if applicable) × (Registration Term).

If purchasing a vehicle, the formula adds the 5.25% Document Fee (calculated on the net purchase price after trade-in), the Title Fee ($35 or $55), and the selected Plate Fee ($10 or $20).

What this calculator may not include

  • Commercial Trucks & Heavy Vehicles: Vehicles exceeding 5,000 lbs in registered weight are subject to an additional $18.00 charge per every 1,000 lbs over the 5,000 lb limit. AFV surcharges also increase significantly for vehicles over 6,000 lbs.
  • Late Renewal Fees: Renewing a vehicle registration after the expiration date incurs an automatic $20.00 late fee.
  • Specialty License Plates: Environmental, animal welfare, or farmland preservation plates require a one-time $50.00 specialty creation fee.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Delaware?

For standard gas-powered passenger cars and trucks under 5,000 lbs, the base registration fee is $40.00 per year. Alternative fuel vehicles must pay a mandatory state surcharge ranging from $60 to $110 per year.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Delaware?

This calculator is accurate for standard passenger cars, SUVs, light pickup trucks weighing 5,000 lbs or less, and motorcycles. It does not calculate specific fees for heavy commercial vehicles, farm trucks, or trailers.

Can registration fees in Delaware change every year?

Delaware's base registration fee is a flat rate and does not depreciate or decrease as the vehicle ages. Fees only change when officially amended by state legislation.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Delaware?

Yes. If you are registering a newly acquired vehicle, select "New Purchase." This will automatically apply the Title Fee and compute your 5.25% Document Fee based on the net purchase price (accounting for any trade-in).
