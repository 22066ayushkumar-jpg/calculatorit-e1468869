How much are vehicle registration fees in South Carolina?

The actual registration fee in South Carolina is one of the lowest in the country at just $40 every two years for standard passenger vehicles. Seniors receive small discounts, lowering the fee to $38 for 64-year-olds and $36 for those 65 or older. However, alternative fuel vehicles carry additional road use fees; battery electric vehicles pay a $120 biennial fee, while hybrids pay $60.

What affects your South Carolina registration fee?

  • Vehicle Owner's Age: Drivers who are 64, 65+, or disabled receive a discounted base registration rate.
  • Alternative Fuel Types: To offset gas tax revenue, South Carolina charges an extra $120 for EVs and $60 for hybrids every two years.
  • Infrastructure Maintenance Fee (IMF): When titling a newly purchased car, you must pay a 5% IMF on the vehicle's price. This fee is capped at a maximum of $500, making South Carolina very tax-friendly for expensive vehicles. If you are moving to SC from another state, the IMF is a flat $250.
  • County Property Tax: You must pay an annual personal property tax to your county treasurer before you can register or renew your vehicle with the SCDMV. This is usually the largest ongoing expense, potentially reaching hundreds of dollars annually.

How are registration fees calculated in South Carolina?

The checkout formula for the SCDMV is:

  • (Biennial Registration Fee) + (Biennial EV/Hybrid Fee) + ($15 Title Fee if applicable) + (IMF if applicable).

Keep in mind that your county property tax must be paid prior to visiting the SCDMV, so it is technically a separate transaction billed by the county.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in South Carolina?

A standard passenger vehicle costs $40 every two years to register.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in South Carolina?

This tool is accurate for standard passenger cars, light trucks under standard weight, and alternative fuel vehicles. It does not apply to heavy commercial vehicles, RVs, motorcycles ($10 fee), or utility trailers ($10 fee).

Can registration fees in South Carolina change every year?

The state's base registration fee is a flat rate that does not depreciate over time, remaining locked at $40 (or the senior rate) every two years. However, your county property tax bill will decrease annually as the vehicle depreciates in value.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in South Carolina?

Yes. By selecting "New Purchase in SC", the calculator will apply the 5% Infrastructure Maintenance Fee, which functions as the state's vehicle sales tax. The calculator automatically caps this fee at the legal maximum of $500.
