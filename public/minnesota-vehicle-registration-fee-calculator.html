How much are vehicle registration fees in Minnesota?

For standard passenger vehicles, Minnesota's registration tabs are calculated heavily on an ad valorem basis, meaning they are tied to the vehicle's original Manufacturer's Suggested Retail Price (MSRP) and its age. First-year registrations are assessed at a rate of 1.575% of the full MSRP. So, a new $40,000 truck will cost over $630 in base registration taxes alone. However, this fee drops systematically as the car gets older, eventually reaching a flat minimum of $20 per year once the vehicle is 11 years old.

What affects your Minnesota registration fee?

  • Original Base Price (MSRP): The higher the factory price of your vehicle when it was brand new, the higher your base registration fee.
  • Vehicle Age: Your registration cost decreases as the vehicle ages over a 10-year depreciation schedule. By year five, you are taxed on 70% of the MSRP. By year ten, you are taxed on just 10%.
  • Universal DVS Fees: Regardless of age, every renewal includes an $8 filing fee, a $1 office fee, and a $2.25 technology surcharge.
  • Powertrain Surcharges: Minnesota imposes an additional annual road usage fee for vehicles that don't pay traditional gas taxes. Fully electric vehicles (BEVs) pay a $150.00 annual surcharge, while plug-in hybrid electric vehicles (PHEVs) pay $75.00.
  • County Wheelage Tax: Most metro counties (like Hennepin and Ramsey) charge an additional $20 wheelage tax to fund local road repairs.
  • Sales Tax (Purchases): Vehicle purchases are subject to a 6.875% state motor vehicle sales tax. Older vehicles (10+ years old) purchased for under $3,000 may qualify for a flat $10 in-lieu tax.

How are registration fees calculated in Minnesota?

The Minnesota DVS calculates your tabs using a highly specific depreciation matrix. The formula is:

  • (MSRP × Depreciation Percentage × 0.01575) + $11.25 Universal Fees + Wheelage Tax + EV Surcharge.

If you are registering a newly purchased car, you must also add the Certificate of Title fee ($8.25), transfer taxes, double license plate fees ($15.50), and the 6.875% sales tax on the purchase price (minus the allowed trade-in credit).

What this calculator may not include

  • Late Registration Penalties: Renewing your tabs after the expiration date carries a penalty based on the overdue period.

  • Specialty & Vanity Plates: Critical habitat plates, personalized plates, and collegiate plates carry additional upfront fees and recurring annual surcharges.

FAQS

How much is the car registration fee in Minnesota?

Registration fees in Minnesota depend strictly on the original base MSRP of the vehicle and its age. A brand new $30,000 vehicle will cost nearly $480 to register in its first year, but that fee will drop continuously until the vehicle reaches 11 years of age, bottoming out at a $20 base fee.

Can registration fees in Minnesota change every year?

Yes. By state law, your registration fee depreciates each year following a specific 10-year bracket. After the 10th year of registration, the fee remains permanently flat.

Why are Minnesota vehicle registration fees so expensive?

Minnesota's ad valorem system ties the registration directly to the value of the vehicle to fund state road infrastructure and highway maintenance. Heavy reliance on these vehicle registration taxes (funding roughly 18% of the transportation budget) eliminates the need for widespread toll roads across the state.
