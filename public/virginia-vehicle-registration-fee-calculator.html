How much are vehicle registration fees in Virginia?

The base registration fee for a standard passenger vehicle weighing 4,000 lbs or less in Virginia is $30.75 per year. Vehicles weighing between 4,001 and 6,500 lbs are charged $35.75 per year. However, your total checkout price will likely be higher because the DMV automatically collects local county registration fees (often $20 to $38) on behalf of your municipality. Furthermore, electric vehicles are subject to an additional flat $135.63 Highway Use Fee annually.

What affects your Virginia registration fee?

  • Vehicle Weight: Virginia organizes its base fees by empty and gross weight brackets. Lighter sedans pay less than heavy SUVs and pickup trucks.
  • Highway Use Fee (HUF): To compensate for lost fuel taxes, Virginia assesses an HUF on fuel-efficient vehicles (25 MPG or greater) and a flat maximum HUF on fully electric vehicles. The EV fee for 2026 is currently indexed at $135.63.
  • Local Registration Fees: The DMV will often collect your local county or city decal fee at the time of your state renewal. For example, the City of Fairfax and Virginia Beach impose their own distinct surcharges.
  • Northern Virginia Emissions Fee: If your vehicle is garaged in an emissions-compliant county (like Arlington, Fairfax, or Loudoun), the DMV applies an extra $2.00 per year administrative fee to your registration.
  • No Trade-In Deductions: If you are purchasing a car, Virginia charges a 4.15% Motor Vehicle Sales and Use Tax based strictly on the gross sales price, or a minimum of $75. Your trade-in allowance does not lower your taxable amount.

How are registration fees calculated in Virginia?

The total DMV invoice is computed using this formula:

  • (Base Weight Fee) + (Highway Use Fee) + (Local Registration Fee) + (Emissions Fee) × Registration Term (1 to 3 Years).

If you are registering a newly purchased car, you must also add the $15 Certificate of Title fee and the 4.15% Sales and Use Tax.

What this calculator may not include

  • Emissions Inspections: The actual emissions test costs up to $28 and is paid directly to the local inspection station. The $2 fee collected by the DMV is purely an administrative surcharge.
  • Personal Property Taxes: Virginia cities and counties levy separate annual personal property taxes on vehicles. This tax is billed and collected directly by your local municipality, not the DMV.
  • Mileage Choice Program: Drivers of EVs or fuel-efficient vehicles who drive infrequently can opt to pay a per-mile road usage fee instead of the upfront annual HUF.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Virginia?

A standard registration for a 4,000 lb passenger car is $30.75 per year. However, local county fees and alternative fuel Highway Use Fees commonly increase the actual renewal bill to between $60 and $200 per year.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Virginia?

This calculator is highly accurate for standard passenger cars, pickup trucks up to 10,000 lbs, and motorcycles. It does not calculate fees for heavy commercial motor carriers, permanent trailer registrations, or rental fleet vehicles.

Can registration fees in Virginia change every year?

Your base weight fee stays locked, but the Highway Use Fee (HUF) is updated by the state every July 1st based on current fuel tax rates and average miles driven by Virginians.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Virginia?

Yes. If you select "New Purchase," the calculator will automatically apply the $15 title fee and compute your 4.15% Sales and Use Tax. Remember that in Virginia, your trade-in does not decrease your taxable sales price.
