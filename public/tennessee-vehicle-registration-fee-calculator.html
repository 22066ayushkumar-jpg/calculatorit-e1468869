How much are vehicle registration fees in Tennessee?

The state of Tennessee charges a flat base registration fee of $29.00 for standard passenger vehicles and $22.00 for motorcycles. However, almost all drivers will pay more than this because the majority of counties add a local "Wheel Tax" (Motor Vehicle Privilege Tax) that ranges from $0 to $75+ per year. Additionally, alternative fuel vehicles face significant annual surcharges.

What affects your Tennessee registration fee?

  • County Wheel Tax: This is the biggest variable in Tennessee. Counties independently set their wheel tax to fund local schools or roads. Davidson County charges $55, Montgomery County charges $74, and some rural counties charge $0.
  • Powertrain Surcharges: Because they pay less in gas tax at the pump, Tennessee mandates annual surcharges for alternative fuel vehicles. Fully electric vehicles (BEVs) pay a $200 surcharge through 2026 (rising to $274 in 2027). Standard hybrids and plug-in hybrids (PHEVs) pay a $100 surcharge.
  • Sales Tax (New Purchases): If titling a vehicle for the first time, you must pay the state sales tax (7.0%) plus your local county tax. Note that Tennessee caps the local portion of the sales tax on the first $1,600 or $3,200 of the purchase price depending on the county, but adds a 2.75% state single article tax on amounts between $1,600 and $3,200.
  • No Emissions Fees: Tennessee officially ended its statewide emissions testing program in January 2022, so there are no longer any inspection fees added to your renewal.

How are registration fees calculated in Tennessee?

Tennessee uses a flat, class-based registration fee with no value, mileage, or age component. Whatever your vehicle is worth, the annual state fee is exactly the same. The calculation formula is straightforward:

  • Formula: State Plate Fee ($29) + Local Clerk Admin Fee (~$2) + County Wheel Tax + EV/Hybrid Surcharge (If applicable).
  • If you are registering a newly purchased car, you must also pay a $14 Title / Owner Change Fee and the applicable state and local sales tax.

What this calculator may not include

  • Specialty & Vanity Plates: Personalized plates or collegiate/charity special background plates carry an additional $35 to $71 fee on top of your base registration.
  • Small Administrative Fees: County clerks often add a minor $2 to $5 processing, postage, or decal handling fee at checkout.
  • Exact Sales Tax Caps: As noted, Tennessee applies a highly specific cap on local sales taxes for vehicles. Because the cap varies dynamically by jurisdiction, the calculator provides a direct percentage-based estimate instead of the strictly capped rate.

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in Tennessee?

The state base fee is only $29.00, but adding your county's wheel tax usually brings the total cost to somewhere between $29.00 and $104.00 per year.

Is this calculator accurate for all vehicles in Tennessee?

This calculator is accurate for standard passenger cars, SUVs, light non-commercial trucks, and motorcycles. It does not calculate specific fees for heavy commercial vehicles, apportioned plates, or low-speed neighborhood vehicles.

Can registration fees in Tennessee change every year?

State base fees are flat and rarely change. However, your county commission may vote to increase or implement a new Wheel Tax at any time. Additionally, electric vehicle surcharges are statutorily scheduled to increase from $200 to $274 on January 1, 2027.

Can I use this calculator for a new car and a used car in Tennessee?

Yes. If you are titling a vehicle for the first time in Tennessee (whether brand new from a dealer or used from a private party), select "New Purchase." This will calculate your title fee and approximate state/local sales taxes.
