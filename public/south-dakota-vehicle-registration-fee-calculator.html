How much are vehicle registration fees in South Dakota?

For standard passenger vehicles in South Dakota, the base registration fee typically ranges from $36.00 to $144.00 per year, depending primarily on the weight of the vehicle . A typical mid-size SUV or sedan weighing between 2,001 and 4,000 lbs will cost $72.00 to register annually . When you register a newly purchased car, you must also pay a 4% Motor Vehicle Excise Tax, a $10 title fee, and an $8 mailing fee for plates .

What affects your South Dakota registration fee?

  • Vehicle Weight: Heavier vehicles cause more road wear and fall into higher fee brackets (scaling roughly $36.00 for every 2,000 lbs) .
  • Vehicle Age: South Dakota rewards long-term ownership. Once your vehicle is 10 years old (based on the model year), you receive a flat 30% discount on your base registration fee .
  • Motor Vehicle Excise Tax (MVET): Instead of a standard local and state sales tax, South Dakota applies a unified 4% MVET on vehicle purchases . Fortunately, trade-in values are fully credited against your taxable purchase price, significantly reducing your burden .
  • Alternative Fuel Surcharges: Fully electric vehicles (BEVs) are assessed a $100 annual fee, while plug-in hybrids (PHEVs) are assessed a $50 annual fee to offset gas tax losses .
  • County Wheel Tax: Individual counties can add a wheel tax up to $60 per vehicle to fund local infrastructure projects .

How are registration fees calculated in South Dakota?

The South Dakota Department of Revenue (DOR) calculates your checkout total using a straightforward weight and age formula :

  • Renewal Formula: (Base Weight Fee) - (30% Age Discount if applicable) + ($4 State Surcharges) + (County Wheel Tax) + (EV Surcharge if applicable) .
  • New Purchase Formula: Renewal Formula + $10 Title Fee + $8 Mailing Fee + (4% × [Purchase Price - Trade In Allowance]) .

What this calculator may not include

Frequently asked questions

How much is the car registration fee in South Dakota?

A typical 4,000-lb passenger vehicle pays $72 per year. This fee drops to $50.40 once the vehicle reaches 10 years of age due to a 30% age discount . County wheel taxes may add between $15 and $60 depending on where you live.

Can registration fees in South Dakota change every year?

Your base weight fee stays locked for the first 9 years of the vehicle's life. Once the vehicle enters its 10th year of registration, South Dakota automatically applies a 30% discount to your base fee, making it permanently cheaper to renew .

Why are South Dakota vehicle registration fees so cheap?

South Dakota boasts one of the lower vehicle tax burdens in the US because it relies on a single 4% Motor Vehicle Excise Tax rather than layering state and local city sales taxes. Furthermore, there are no annual ad valorem property taxes, and no required inspections or emissions testing .
